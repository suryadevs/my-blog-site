## History

This blog began it's life in early 2015, as a way to put my thoughts on digital paper. I used to write things on actual paper but then things used to get lost. So I decided to do this digitally so that I don't lose things later.


## Topics
Most of the articles are just my [personal](/categories/personal/) thoughts. Feel free to agree or disagree with them.

Over the years I have also delved into writing other things than just my thoughts. During Covid I found free time to write a short story which I have included in the [stories](/categories/stories/) category in this blog.

I also work with computers a lot in my day job, so I also have some articles on that [tech aspect](/categories/tech/) of my life.


## Languages

No, I don't mean computer languages. I have written articles and poems mostly in **English** and **Marathi** languages. A few are in **Hindi** language.

That said I do know a few computer languages but I am not as good at them as spoken languages.


## Social

If you want to let me know your thoughts and just want to contact me you can reach out to me on Mastodon or LinkedIN. Links are on the top right of this page.


## In closing

I hope you enjoy this blog as much as I have enjoyed writing over the years.
